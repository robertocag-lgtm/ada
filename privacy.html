Privacy policy

Ada is a private assistant used only by the members of a single household.

With the account owner's permission, Ada reads and sends email and manages calendar events in that owner's Google account, solely to carry out the owner's requests.

Google user data is stored only on the household's own server. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

Ada's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
